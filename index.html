Common Questions

Is a consultation required before treatment?

Yes. A short consultation lets our team understand your skin and goals and recommend the right treatment.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment, your skin and your goals. We will give you an honest estimate at your consultation.

Are the treatments painful?

Most people find them comfortable. Sensation varies by treatment and person, and we will explain what to expect beforehand.

Is there downtime?

Many treatments, such as HydraFacial, have little or none. We will explain aftercare for your specific treatment.
